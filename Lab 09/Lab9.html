THE MUGHAL EMPIRE

A Legacy of Art, Architecture, and Power (1526–1857)

The Dawn of an Era

Founded by Babur in 1526 after the First Battle of Panipat, the Mughal Empire extended over most of the Indian subcontinent. It is renowned for its unique Indo-Persian culture and architectural wonders like the Taj Mahal.
